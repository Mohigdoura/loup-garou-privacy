Privacy Policy for Loup Garou Game

Last Updated: February 3, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains how Loup Garou ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Information We Collect

We do NOT collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

The app only uses local storage on your device to save:

  • Player names entered during gameplay
  • Game preferences and settings

This information is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party.

How We Use Information

The locally stored player names are used solely to:

  • Remember player names between game sessions
  • Improve user experience by avoiding re-entering names
  • No other purpose

Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device using local storage
  • Data never leaves your device
  • We have no access to this information
  • You can clear this data at any time by clearing the app's cache/data or uninstalling the app

Third-Party Services

Our app does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or data collection services, with the exception of:

  • Google Play Services - Required for app distribution
  • AdMob (if applicable) - For displaying advertisements

If ads are present, ad providers may collect certain information for ad personalization. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect any information from children or any users. Since we store data only locally on the device and collect no personal information, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Data Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted, the security of your information depends on your device's security measures. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device secure with a password/PIN
  • Only downloading apps from official sources
  • Keeping your device's operating system updated

Your Rights

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: You can view player names stored in the app at any time
  • Deletion: You can delete all locally stored data by clearing the app's cache or uninstalling the app
  • Portability: Since data never leaves your device, you maintain full control

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
